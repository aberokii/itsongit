Within-day (B − A), 6 days: skip -3.8 pts, views -110. B had the lower skip rate on 3 of 6 days.
Median skip difference B − A: 95% interval -15.5 to +0.8 pts (negative = B better).
**Verdict: keep testing** (13 A / 12 B; decide at 15 each). Rule: B wins if its median skip rate is at least 5 points lower than A's AND its median views are not lower. A wins if B is not better after 15 per arm. Then make the winner the default and test the next hook idea against it.
