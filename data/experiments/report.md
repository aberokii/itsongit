Within-day (B − A), 3 days: skip -6.9 pts, views -75. B had the lower skip rate on 1 of 3 days.
Median skip difference B − A: 95% interval -19.9 to +2.6 pts (negative = B better).
**Verdict: keep testing** (8 A / 8 B; decide at 15 each). Rule: B wins if its median skip rate is at least 5 points lower than A's AND its median views are not lower. A wins if B is not better after 15 per arm. Then make the winner the default and test the next hook idea against it.
