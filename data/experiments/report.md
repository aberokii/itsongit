## Format test: Does the animated story format (Kit the cat, beats, whip pans) beat the classic ad template?

| format | n | median views | median skip % | median watch s | shares+saves /100 views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| classic | 15 | 131 | 71.7 | 5.4 | 1.5 |
| story | 17 | 149 | 63.6 | 5.8 | 2.1 |

**Verdict: classic wins** (median views classic 131 vs story 149, skip 71.7 vs 63.6). story wins if its median views beat classic by 30%+ with no worse skip rate: then it becomes 2 of 3 daily reels (and 3 of 3 if it wins again over the next 12). Classic wins if story isn't ahead after 12 each.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| price | 9 | 135 | 71.7 | 5.4 | 1.5 |

Within-day (B − A), 3 days: skip -4.5 pts, views -75. B had the lower skip rate on 1 of 3 days.
Median skip difference B − A: 95% interval -17.2 to +3.0 pts (negative = B better).
**Verdict: keep testing** (9 A / 8 B; decide at 15 each). Rule: B wins if its median skip rate is at least 5 points lower than A's AND its median views are not lower. A wins if B is not better after 15 per arm. Then make the winner the default and test the next hook idea against it.
