## Format test: Does the animated story format (Kit the cat, beats, whip pans) beat the classic ad template?

| format | n | median views | median skip % | median watch s | shares+saves /100 views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| classic | 12 | 125.0 | 72.2 | 5.3 | 1.6 |
| story | 14 | 140.5 | 66.3 | 5.2 | 1.8 |

**Verdict: classic wins** (median views classic 125.0 vs story 140.5, skip 72.2 vs 66.3). story wins if its median views beat classic by 30%+ with no worse skip rate: then it becomes 2 of 3 daily reels (and 3 of 3 if it wins again over the next 12). Classic wins if story isn't ahead after 12 each.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| price | 7 | 129 | 72.7 | 5.4 | 1.6 |

Within-day (B − A), 2 days: skip -10.4 pts, views -94. B had the lower skip rate on 1 of 2 days.
Median skip difference B − A: 95% interval -20.5 to +2.4 pts (negative = B better).
**Verdict: keep testing** (7 A / 7 B; decide at 15 each). Rule: B wins if its median skip rate is at least 5 points lower than A's AND its median views are not lower. A wins if B is not better after 15 per arm. Then make the winner the default and test the next hook idea against it.
